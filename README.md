# Ola-Community-Impact-Rides
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)
#### Product Space Fellowship — Cohort Project
#### ⭐ Recognized as one of the best submissions of the cohort

## 📌 Overview
Community Impact Rides is a product concept designed to integrate social impact directly into Ola's ride-booking experience.
The feature enables riders to support verified local causes through optional micro-donations, while providing transparency into how their contributions create measurable community impact.
The concept aims to make social contribution simple, transparent, and seamlessly integrated into an everyday activity: taking a ride.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
While ride-sharing platforms make urban transportation increasingly accessible, users currently have limited opportunities to connect their everyday rides with causes they care about.

The proposed solution addresses:
- Lack of direct access to community-focused initiatives
- Limited transparency around social impact
- Friction in discovering and supporting local causes
- Missed opportunities to turn everyday transactions into meaningful contributions

## 💡 Proposed Solution
Community Impact Rides introduces an optional donation experience within the existing ride journey.

Users can:
- Discover verified local causes
- Select a cause they care abou
- Contribute a small amount or percentage of their fare
- Track the progress of supported initiatives
- View their personal impact over time
- Earn badges and recognition for continued participation

## 👤 Target User
The primary persona is the Socially Conscious Millennial — a digitally savvy urban professional who wants to contribute to social causes but prefers convenient, low-effort ways to make an impact.

The PRD also explores:
- Busy Working Parents
- Eco-Conscious Young Professionals
- Community Volunteers

## 🧭 Core User Flow
#### Ride Selection → Cause Selection → Donation → Ride Completion → Impact Summary
The experience is designed to keep donations optional and lightweight, without disrupting the core ride-booking journey.
